# Market Analysis Basics

Market analysis is the process of examining financial markets to make informed investment decisions. There are two primary approaches: fundamental analysis (examining company and economic data) and technical analysis (studying price charts and trading patterns). Understanding both helps investors develop a complete view of market dynamics.

## Fundamental Analysis

Fundamental analysis evaluates a security's intrinsic value by examining underlying economic, financial, and qualitative factors.

### Company-Level Analysis

**Income Statement Analysis:**
- Revenue growth: Is the company growing its top line?
- Gross margin: Revenue minus cost of goods sold
- Operating margin: Efficiency after operating expenses
- Net income margin: Bottom-line profitability
- Earnings Per Share (EPS): Net income divided by shares outstanding

**Balance Sheet Analysis:**
- Current ratio (current assets ÷ current liabilities): Liquidity assessment
- Debt-to-equity ratio: Financial leverage
- Return on equity (ROE): How efficiently management uses equity
- Free cash flow: Cash generated after capital expenditures

**Key Valuation Metrics:**
- **P/E Ratio (Price-to-Earnings):** Most widely used valuation metric; compare to industry peers and historical averages
- **PEG Ratio:** P/E divided by earnings growth rate; accounts for growth; under 1.0 may indicate undervaluation
- **EV/EBITDA:** Enterprise value to earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization; useful for comparing companies with different debt levels
- **Price-to-Sales (P/S):** Useful for companies with no earnings
- **Price-to-Book (P/B):** Compares market price to accounting book value

### Macroeconomic Analysis

**Economic Indicators:**
- **GDP Growth:** Measures overall economic expansion or contraction
- **Inflation (CPI, PCE):** Rising inflation affects company margins and Federal Reserve policy
- **Unemployment Rate:** Low unemployment signals economic strength but can lead to wage inflation
- **Consumer Confidence:** Leading indicator of consumer spending
- **PMI (Purchasing Managers' Index):** Above 50 indicates expansion; below 50 indicates contraction

**Federal Reserve Policy:**
- Interest rate decisions dramatically affect all asset classes
- Rate hikes: Tighten financial conditions; typically negative for stocks, especially growth stocks
- Rate cuts: Loosen financial conditions; typically positive for stocks and bonds

## Technical Analysis

Technical analysis studies historical price and volume data to identify patterns and trends, operating on the premise that market psychology repeats itself in recognizable patterns.

**Key Concepts:**
- **Support levels:** Price points where buying interest historically emerges
- **Resistance levels:** Price points where selling pressure historically appears
- **Trend lines:** Lines connecting successive highs or lows showing market direction
- **Moving averages:** Smoothed price data (50-day, 200-day MA are widely watched)
- **Volume:** Confirms price moves; rising price with rising volume is bullish

**Common Indicators:**
- **RSI (Relative Strength Index):** Oscillator measuring momentum; above 70 is overbought, below 30 is oversold
- **MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence):** Trend-following momentum indicator
- **Bollinger Bands:** Price channels showing volatility and potential breakouts

## Sector Rotation

Different sectors of the economy perform differently at various stages of the economic cycle:
- **Early expansion:** Financials, consumer discretionary, real estate outperform
- **Mid-cycle:** Technology, industrials, materials
- **Late cycle:** Energy, healthcare, consumer staples
- **Recession:** Utilities, healthcare, consumer staples (defensive sectors)

## Market Sentiment Indicators

- **Fear & Greed Index:** CNN's composite of 7 indicators measuring market emotion
- **VIX (Volatility Index):** "Fear gauge"—above 30 indicates high fear; below 15 suggests complacency
- **Put/Call Ratio:** High ratio indicates more puts than calls (bearish sentiment)
- **AAII Investor Sentiment Survey:** Weekly survey of individual investor bull/bear positioning
